# Memorandum of Association
## 1. Name and form of the company
The proposed name of the company is Imarah Technologies (Private) Limited. It will trade under the name Imarah.tech. The proposed form is a private company limited by shares in Pakistan. Name approval and incorporation have not yet been obtained.

This memorandum is prepared for Assignment 1 as an academic startup proposal. The founders' names come from the supplied project proposal. The company name, capital, ownership and registered office arrangements below are planning assumptions, subject to the founders' agreement and the applicable incorporation requirements.

## 2. Registered office
The registered office is proposed to be situated in the Province of Sindh, Pakistan, with operations based in Karachi. The complete office address will be selected before incorporation. The university campus is not being represented as the company's registered office.

## 3. Principal line of business
The company will develop and provide software that helps engineering teams, building managers and construction firms record inspections, review visible defects and manage project information. Its first product will be a web and mobile-accessible inspection application that combines photographs, crack segmentation, model-generated observations and human review in one report.

The purpose is to make inspection records easier to prepare, compare and follow up. The software will support a professional's work. It will not certify structural safety or replace an examination by a qualified engineer.

## 4. Main business objectives
4.1 Develop, test, maintain and license the Imarah.tech application for building inspection and construction record management.

4.2 Provide tools for uploading photographs, outlining visible cracks, preparing draft observations and maintaining an inspection history with reviewer notes.

4.3 Develop construction management features for worker and contractor records, attendance, protective equipment observations, material usage and progress reporting after suitable validation.

4.4 Research and develop building monitoring features using prototype sensors and weather information, and area-level analysis using permitted satellite and geospatial data.

4.5 Provide product setup, user training, technical support and agreed software integrations for customers using the platform.

4.6 Evaluate the software and models using documented methods, record their limitations and improve the product using feedback from users and relevant professionals.

# Business scope and capital
## 5. Products and services
The initial commercial offering is proposed to be a subscription for an inspection workspace, supported by a one-time onboarding service. The workspace will bring together the original photograph, detector output, model observations and the customer's review. A subscription will pay for software access and support, not a structural safety certificate.

Construction management, worker safety monitoring, sensor dashboards and urban analysis are future product areas. At the date of this document, their screens in the September prototype use illustrative data. They will be offered as working services only after their data sources, permissions, models and operating procedures have been tested.

## 6. Activities supporting the business
Within its lawful business scope, the company may develop and acquire software, obtain suitable model and dataset licences, rent computing services, engage employees and advisers, enter customer and supplier agreements, and protect its own intellectual property.

The company may collaborate with universities, engineering professionals, contractors and technology providers through written agreements. No existing partnership, customer contract or endorsement is claimed in this memorandum.

The company may raise finance or borrow for its own business, subject to applicable law and the approvals in its articles. It will not invite public deposits or offer regulated financial services through this provision.

## 7. Boundaries of the business
Payroll and insurance features will record information only. They will not transfer salaries, provide insurance or arrange regulated financial transactions. Worker monitoring will require a separately approved privacy and access policy before real personal data is collected.

The company will not claim that a photograph establishes a building's structural condition. Model output will remain distinguishable from a qualified person's assessment. Any activity requiring a professional licence or regulatory permission will begin only after the relevant requirement is met.

## 8. Liability of members
The liability of each member is limited to the amount, if any, unpaid on the shares held by that member, subject to applicable law. This clause does not remove obligations that may arise separately from personal guarantees, misconduct or duties imposed by law.

## 9. Share capital
The proposed authorised share capital is PKR 60,000, divided into 600 ordinary shares of PKR 100 each. The proposed initial subscription is 450 ordinary shares, representing PKR 45,000. Each founder is proposed to subscribe for 150 shares and contribute PKR 15,000 over one year.

The annual contribution is PKR 15,000 per founder, not a monthly payment. For cash-flow planning, each founder contributes PKR 1,250 monthly, or PKR 3,750 monthly across the team. Receipts will be recorded as they are paid. The proposed share subscription is not a claim that all shares are already fully paid; allotment and payment terms will be agreed before incorporation.

The remaining 150 authorised shares are unissued and carry no voting or dividend rights while unissued. Future allotments will follow the articles and applicable law. The one-year contribution follows the team's stated budget; it is not a statement that money has already been received.
